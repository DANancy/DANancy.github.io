Business Context

The Renewable Energy Target scheme requires liable entities — electricity retailers and large energy users — to surrender Large-scale Generation Certificates (LGCs) and Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs) each year to cover their liability. When an entity fails to surrender enough certificates, a shortfall is recorded and a shortfall charge may apply. The Clean Energy Regulator publishes this as the public Certificate Shortfall Register, updated twice yearly.
