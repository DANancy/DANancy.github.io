Business Context

The Renewable Energy Certificate (REC) market supports Australia's clean energy transition. Liable entities must surrender certificates (LGCs or STCs) to meet their obligations. When they fall short, a shortfall charge applies. This project delivers insights into shortfalls, refunds, and trends to support better decision making.
